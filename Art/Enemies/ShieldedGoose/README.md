# Shielded Goose artwork

Current assembly: the approved green Basic Goose 3 body, reused unchanged, under a round transparent cyan bubble. Current v3 adds a much thinner outline and spherical pixel shading: a curved upper-left reflection, broad stepped bands and a deeper lower-right crescent. The membrane covers the pilot, hull and thruster while leaving them visible. No body regeneration or duplicate body texture is needed.

## Visual setup

```text
ShieldedGooseVisual
  Body               existing BasicGoose3 sprite; sorting order 0
  Shield             disable this whole group when the shield is consumed
    Membrane         v3 spherical sprite; sorting order 1; renderer color alpha 0.32
    Rim              v3 thin sprite; sorting order 2; renderer color alpha 1
```

The v3 source's center alpha is 252/255. SpriteRenderer alpha 0.32 reduces the center to approximately 32% opacity. The membrane's curved color bands provide volume while the separate fine rim outlines the sphere. Both PNGs retain their original generated pixels; opacity and fitting use native renderer/Transform settings. Use the prefab's two layers rather than placing `Shield.png` alone at full opacity.

Body PPU remains 516.838487972509 and its visible width remains 2.2734375 world units. Shield layers use PPU 339.8937831927525 and centered pivots. Their independent native scales and offsets align the generated visible bounds into a circle 3.0009375 world units across both axes, 132% of the body width. The Shield group is centered on the body's visible bounds at approximately (0.000967420, -0.047403590, 0). Exact child scales/offsets are in `assembly.json`. The body, root and Shield group retain scale 1; all rotations are identity. Imports use Sprite/Single, Point filtering, Clamp, Full Rect, real alpha, no mipmaps, compression or NPOT resizing. Both 1254-pixel canvases fit the 2048 import limit. Existing asset GUIDs and sprite IDs are preserved.

## Gameplay handoff

README and current Shielded Goose data specify 4 HP, 1 money, 1 fuel and 1 single-target damage, with the first incoming ordinary hit negated by its shield. Existing `BaseEnemyData.TakeDamage` consumes `HasShield` and calls the currently empty `OnShieldBroken()` hook; shield-ignoring damage bypasses that step. This art task changes no gameplay scripts, stats, data assignments or scene wiring.

Future gameplay must disable `Shield` when the shield is consumed and restore it from `HasShield` on initialization/reuse. Do not hide it on shield-bypassing damage. Static removal communicates the spent state; a short break animation is optional and has not been generated. No flight frames are needed for the current UFO. This prefab contains only Transform and SpriteRenderer components and cannot be assigned directly to the typed gameplay-prefab field in EnemyData.

Pilot faces local -X and the underside points local -Y. Current enemy gameplay roots face +Y inward, so a future visual child needs local Z rotation 180 degrees for the underside to face the planet. Reuse `Assets/Prefabs/Projectiles/GoosePoopProjectileVisual.prefab` and `GoosePoopImpactVisual.prefab`; poop art faces +X and needs a +90-degree visual-child offset under the current +Y-forward EnemyProjectile root. Projectile spawn alignment, firing and pooling remain gameplay work.
